I've been writing technical articles for years, and the most frustrating part has never been the writing — it's the publishing. After finishing an article, I'd spend another hour opening 11 different tabs, copy-pasting my content, re-uploading images, and fixing formatting on each platform.

So I built something to fix it.

**FaXia** is a free, open-source tool that takes your Markdown file and publishes it to 11+ platforms simultaneously. Write once, publish everywhere — literally.

It supports: GitHub Pages, WeChat, Zhihu, Juejin, Toutiao, Weibo, Reddit, X/Twitter, Medium, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

What I love about it:
- Your credentials and content stay on your computer — there's no cloud service, no account, no tracking
- Images auto-upload to each platform's hosting, so nothing breaks
- It remembers where you published, so re-publishing updates existing posts instead of creating duplicates
- You can use it from the terminal (`tdp publish my-article.md`) or the desktop app

It's completely free and open source under the MIT license. If you write content across multiple platforms, this will save you hours every week.
